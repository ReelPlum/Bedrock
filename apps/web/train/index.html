Saving your samples

What is saved. The pen strokes you write on these pages, the word or formula you were asked to write, the language, the kind of device (for example "ipad") and whether you used a pen, a finger or a mouse. If you choose to say, whether you are left or right handed.

Words from your notes, only if you turn it on. With "Send every word I write in notes" on in the app's settings, each handwritten word you write in a note from then on is sent: its pen strokes, moved so where on the page it was is not sent, and what the app read it as. When you say a reading is right, correct it, or say it is not a word, that is sent too. Nothing else from the note is sent: no typed text, pictures or titles. Turning it off stops it at once.

PDFs you import. When you import a PDF of handwritten notes (for example from GoodNotes) into the app, each handwritten word in it is sent the same way, with the text the PDF says it reads as, to be checked before it is used.

What is not. No name, no email address and no account. You are a random number made on this device.

What it is for. Training Bedrock's handwriting recognition, and nothing else. The samples are kept private on Cloudflare in the EU and are not shared or sold.

Changing your mind. "Delete my contributions" below removes everything you sent.
